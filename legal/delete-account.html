Delete your OneDevs account

OneDevs, by Mohameds Engineering and Build Studio, Freetown, Sierra Leone

In the app

  1. Open OneDevs and sign in.
  2. Tap your picture in the top corner to open Profile.
  3. Scroll to Your data and tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be deleted and tap Delete.

Your account is deleted straight away and you are signed out.

Without the app

Email mebssoftware9@gmail.com from the Google account you sign in to OneDevs with, with the subject Delete my OneDevs account. We delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, picture and sign-in.
  • Apps you listed, their icons and testing instructions.
  • Your DevCoin balance and history.
  • Tests, missions, check-ins and results.
  • Feedback reports you sent, badges, your plan record and the apps chosen in the Lab.
  • Device checks recorded for your account.

What is kept

  • Chat messages you sent inside a mission stay visible to the other members of that mission, with your name removed.
  • Backups of our database are overwritten in the normal cycle and are gone within 30 days of deletion.
  • Google Play keeps its own records of purchases and subscriptions. Deleting your OneDevs account does not cancel a Premium or Pro subscription: cancel it in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
