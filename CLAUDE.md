# Working with the owner

Never leave the owner a list of tasks, in a README or anywhere else. Do every
step that can be done from the repo or the container. When a step needs the
owner's own accounts (Play Console, Google Cloud, Supabase dashboard), walk
them through it in the chat one step at a time, and wait for them to finish it
before giving the next one.

READMEs describe how things work. They are not to-do lists.

# Visual system

Hierarchy comes from fill, never from outlines. Every screen sits on
`oneDevsColors.page`; every section, group, list row and panel is a filled,
rounded `oneDevsColors.card`; a row or field inside a card is
`oneDevsColors.well`. No hairline borders or dividers. A selected choice is
`brandTint`, not a ring. Hero cards (the Board's live card, the Launch
actions, plan promos) use their accent or the brand sky as a gradient.
